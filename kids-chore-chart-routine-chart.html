A chore chart and routine chart for kids aged 3 to 7

Getting a small child to brush teeth, finish a meal or tidy toys should not be a battle every day. A short routine, the same order every day, and a small cheer the moment each step is done usually works better than a long list and a reward at the end of the week. Below: tasks by age, a morning and bedtime routine to copy, how to keep rewards gentle, and how Lumi Mission Planet fits in.

Which tasks fit which age

AgeRoutine tasksSimple chores
3–4Brush teeth with help, wash hands, finish a mealPut toys in a basket, put shoes by the door
5–6Get dressed, pack a school bag with a checklistSet napkins on the table, feed a pet with an adult
6–7Morning routine with fewer reminders, bedtime routineMake the bed, sort laundry by color

These are general ideas. Start with two or three tasks your child can already almost do, and add one more only when those feel easy.

A morning and bedtime routine you can copy

Morning: wake up, toilet, wash face, get dressed, breakfast, brush teeth, shoes on, bag ready.

Bedtime: tidy toys, bath or wash, pajamas, brush teeth, one book, lights out.

Keep the order the same every day. Children can follow a routine they can predict, and you can point to the next step instead of repeating instructions.

Keeping rewards gentle

  • Cheer right away. Praise the moment a step is done, not only at the end of the day.
  • Praise effort, not perfection. “You put every block back” is clearer than “good job”.
  • Keep it small. A sticker or a star is enough; the routine itself should become the habit.
  • Do it together. Sit with your child for the first weeks and celebrate each finished mission.

Frequently asked questions

What age is a chore chart good for?

Many families start around age three with one or two tasks, such as putting toys in a basket. Follow your child’s pace rather than a fixed age.

Should I use stickers or screen rewards?

Either can work if the reward is small and immediate. In Lumi Mission Planet, each tap gets an instant cheer and stickers are collected over time.
