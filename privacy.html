01 What we store, and where

The app stores your child's progress — completed tasks, streaks, growth stage, collected stickers and achievements, the chosen buddy character, your custom tasks, settings, and the local timestamps of each completion — only on your device, using on-device local storage. This information is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and it is removed when you delete the app. An optional "Export CSV" button in the parent area lets you save a copy of the completion log as a plain text file; that file is created locally and goes only where you choose to send it. We never receive it. The optional "Export backup" and "Import backup" buttons work the same way: the backup file is created on your device and shared only through the system share sheet — the iOS share sheet or the Android share menu — to a destination you choose, so you can move your progress to a new device. If you enter an optional nickname for your child, it is used only to personalise greetings and reward cards inside the app, it stays on your device, and you can change or remove it at any time in the parent area. App 僅在裝置本機(on-device local storage)儲存孩子的進度(已完成任務、連續天數、成長階段、收集的貼紙與成就、選擇的夥伴角色、你的自訂任務、設定,以及每次完成的本地時間)。此資料不會傳送給我們或任何第三方,並在你刪除 App 時一併移除。家長區的「匯出 CSV」按鈕可把完成紀錄存成純文字檔;「匯出備份/匯入備份」也一樣——備份檔在本機產生,只透過系統分享面板(iOS 分享面板或 Android 分享選單)送到你自己選擇的地方(例如換新手機時還原進度),我們不會收到。若你選填孩子的小名,它只用於 App 內的問候與獎勵卡片個人化,僅儲存於裝置本機,且隨時可在家長區修改或移除。

02 No data collection, no ads, no tracking

Lumi Mission Planet and Lumi Mission Planet Pro do not collect, use, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no behavioral profiling, no analytics, no attribution, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Our App Privacy label on the App Store is therefore "Data Not Collected," and the Google Play Data safety section likewise declares that no user data is collected or shared. 「Lumi 任務星球」與「Lumi 任務星球 Pro」不蒐集、不使用、不傳輸、不販售、不分享任何個人資料。沒有帳號、登入、廣告、行為分析、數據分析、歸因,也沒有任何第三方追蹤。因此 App Store 上的隱私標籤為「未蒐集資料(Data Not Collected)」,Google Play 的「資料安全性」也同樣申報不收集、不分享任何使用者資料。

03 Device permissions

Photos (optional) — only used if you tap "Save Card" to save a victory picture to your own photo library. The app only adds an image you asked it to save; it never reads, browses, or uploads your photos. Notifications (optional) — only used for the gentle daily reminder you turn on yourself in the Parent Dashboard. Reminders are scheduled locally on the device; no data leaves the phone. Both permissions are entirely optional and the app works fully without them. The Android edition behaves the same way: the notification permission is only requested when you switch the daily reminder on yourself (Android 13 and later), and photo access is only requested at the moment you ask the app to save a picture. Apart from those, the Android edition uses internet access only so that Google Play can process a purchase or show the optional rating prompt, plus vibration for gentle haptic feedback; it never uses the camera, microphone, location or contacts. 相簿(選用)——僅在你點「儲存卡片」把勝利圖存到自己的相簿時使用;App 只會新增你要求儲存的圖片,絕不讀取、瀏覽或上傳你的照片。通知(選用)——僅用於你自己在家長後台開啟的每日提醒,提醒在裝置本機排程,沒有任何資料離開手機。兩種權限皆為選用,不開啟也能完整使用 App。Android 版行為相同:通知權限僅在你自己開啟每日提醒時才要求(Android 13 以上),相簿權限僅在你要求 App 儲存圖片的當下才要求。除此之外,Android 版的網路連線僅供 Google Play 處理購買與顯示選用的評分提示,另使用震動提供輕微的觸覺回饋,從不使用相機、麥克風、定位或通訊錄。

04 Third-party software we use

The app is built with the open-source Expo and React Native frameworks (including their standard on-device modules for audio, haptics, local storage, blur effects, image capture, language detection, and local notifications). It contains no advertising, analytics, attribution, social-media, or crash-reporting SDKs — for example, no Google AdMob, no Firebase Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no Sentry. These components run locally to display the app; they do not collect, transmit, or sell your child's personal data. 本 App 以開源的 Expo 與 React Native 框架開發(含其標準本機模組:音訊、觸覺回饋、本機儲存、毛玻璃效果、影像擷取、語言偵測、本機通知等)。不含任何廣告、分析、歸因、社群或當機回報 SDK——例如沒有 Google AdMob、沒有 Firebase Analytics、沒有 Facebook SDK、沒有 Sentry。這些元件僅在本機運作以顯示畫面,不會蒐集、傳輸或販售孩子的個人資料。

05 Purchases

Lumi Mission Planet (free edition) offers a single one-time, non-consumable purchase to unlock the full content; there are no subscriptions and no consumable purchases. Lumi Mission Planet Pro is a paid-up-front app and contains no in-app purchases at all. In both cases any transaction is handled entirely by the Apple App Store on iPhone and iPad, and entirely by Google Play on Android; we never receive or store your payment information, card number, Apple Account or Google Account. 「Lumi 任務星球」(免費版)提供單一的一次性買斷型內購以解鎖完整內容,沒有訂閱、沒有消耗型購買。「Lumi 任務星球 Pro」為買斷制 App,內部完全沒有任何內購。兩者的交易在 iPhone 與 iPad 上皆完全由 Apple App Store 處理,在 Android 上則完全由 Google Play 處理;我們不會收到或儲存你的付款資訊、信用卡號、Apple 帳號或 Google 帳號。

06 Children's privacy

The app is designed for young children (around ages 3 to 7) to use together with a parent. Because it collects no personal information at all and contains no third-party ads or tracking, it complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU GDPR-K. Actions that lead outside the app, and the one-time purchase in the free edition, are placed behind a parental long-press gate. 本 App 為幼兒(約 3 到 7 歲)設計,由家長陪同使用。因完全不蒐集個人資訊、且無任何第三方廣告或追蹤,符合美國 COPPA 與歐盟 GDPR-K。任何會離開 App 的動作,以及免費版的一次性購買,都設有家長長按關卡。

For parents: children's privacy at a glance 給家長：兒童隱私重點

This is a summary of the policy above. It adds no new practice and does not change it. 以下是上方政策的重點整理，沒有新增或變更任何做法。

What information is collected from my child?

None. The app does not ask for, collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information from children or parents. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Learning progress and settings, and anything your child creates or enters in the app, stay only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. 沒有。App 不會向孩子或家長要求、蒐集、傳送、販售或分享任何個人資訊；沒有帳號、不需登入。學習進度、設定，以及孩子在 App 內建立或輸入的內容，都只存在你的裝置上，刪除 App 時一併移除。

Advertising and tracking

No advertising of any kind, no behavioral advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not track your child across other companies' apps or websites. 沒有任何廣告、沒有行為式廣告、沒有數據分析、沒有追蹤，也沒有會蒐集資料的第三方 SDK；App 不會跨其他公司的 App 或網站追蹤孩子。

COPPA and GDPR-K

Because no personal information is collected from children, no parental consent for data collection is needed, nothing is disclosed to third parties, and the app is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the children's provisions of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K). Purchases and links that lead outside the app are meant for parents and are handled as described above. 由於不向兒童蒐集任何個人資訊，因此不需要為資料蒐集取得家長同意，也沒有任何資料揭露給第三方；本 App 依美國《兒童線上隱私保護法》（COPPA）與歐盟 GDPR 兒童條款（GDPR-K）設計。購買與離開 App 的連結是給家長使用，處理方式如上方政策所述。

Your rights as a parent

We hold no personal information about you or your child, so there is nothing on our side to review, export or delete. You can erase everything the app stored at any time by deleting the app from the device. If you have any question or request about your child's privacy, contact us and we will reply. 我們沒有保存任何關於你或孩子的個人資訊，因此在我們這端沒有可供查閱、匯出或刪除的資料。你隨時可以從裝置刪除 App，以清除 App 儲存的所有內容。若對孩子的隱私有任何問題或要求，歡迎與我們聯絡，我們會回覆。
