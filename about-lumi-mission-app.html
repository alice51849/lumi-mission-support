1. What it is

Lumi Mission: Reward Chart is a reward and routine app for young children: the child taps for every small win in a daily mission, and a bunny buddy jumps, cheers and grows along.

  • Daily missions for meals, tooth brushing, sleep time, tidy-up and school prep
  • Six buddy characters to collect: bunny, bear, kitten, panda, dinosaur and fox
  • One hundred achievement stickers across missions, streaks and perfect days
  • Parent dashboard with today at a glance, a seven-day calendar and a per-tap log that can be exported as CSV
  • Daily reminder, backup export and import, and 44 languages

2. Who it is for

Parents of children aged about three to seven, designed to be used together. Typical moments: getting teeth brushed, finishing meals, tidying toys and winding down for bed. There is no account, no ads and no tracking; everything stays on the device.

3. How it is paid for

The download is free. Meals, tooth brushing, sleep and tidy-up missions, the bunny buddy and the first stickers in every category are included without a purchase. A single one-time purchase unlocks the school prep mission, custom missions, all six buddies and the full sticker collection. There is no subscription.
